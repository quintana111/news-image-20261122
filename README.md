One image summing up Sunday 22 November 2026 in world news: a cream
broadsheet front page with 48 stories — three leads (COP31 closing in on a
deal at dawn in Antalya, the Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze in the Gulf
of Finland, Iran claiming Patriot radar strikes in Bahrain), twelve
illustrated tiles (Bitcoin back above $60,000, France seeking UN action on
Myanmar, missiles hitting Zelenskyy's hometown, the capture of cartel boss
"El Chapo Isidro" in Sinaloa, two tankers hit off Oman, the Basra
power-plant fire, four killed in Gaza, Trump's twenty-year Iran deal, more
Ukraine ceasefires, Hegseth's new limits on Canada ties, a terror warning
for US airlines, England thrashing the All Blacks 37-12), a dotted world
map with 15 numbered pins, a UTC timeline, BY FIELD / BY REGION count
strips, a day-in-numbers strip, and a 33-item "Also" list. Every headline
and figure comes from the day's reporting (Reuters, AP, CNN, AFP, Al
Jazeera, BBC, USGS, CoinDesk, Fox News); a DATE CHECK box names the earlier
stories deliberately left out. The image is rendered deterministically with
Python PIL and contains no AI-generated art.

## Run

```sh
# Re-render the front page (PIL only; deterministic — two runs are byte-identical)
python3 ../news-image-20261122-work/compose.py

# Copy the entry image to /output/entry.png with sha256 verification
node main.ts
```
